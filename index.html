Hermes Workspace Agent

A private, single-user automation agent.

What this is

Hermes Workspace Agent is a personal automation tool. It connects to Google Workspace APIs on behalf of one individual user — its owner — so that an assistant running on that user's own computer can read, search, draft and send their mail, and work with their calendar, drive, contacts, documents and spreadsheets on request.

It is not a service, it has no other users, and it is not offered to the public. It runs locally on the owner's machine.

Google account access

Access is granted by the owner through Google's standard OAuth consent flow, and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The scopes requested, and the reason for each, are listed in the Privacy Policy.
